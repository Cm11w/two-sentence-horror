Entry for the [Two-Sentence Horror](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253) bounty on make.fun: one original horror story told in exactly two sentences, judged pairwise by a house judge.

## The story

> I have lived alone for eleven years, and every morning the kettle is already warm when I come downstairs. Last night I stayed awake to catch whoever makes it, and at dawn the kettle clicked off behind me.

204 characters, exactly two sentences. The comfort of the first sentence — a warm kettle every morning — turns on the second: someone has been inside the house all along, and now they are standing behind the narrator.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the story to `/output/entry.txt`. It self-checks the competition gate first: exactly two sentences, 1–400 characters, not only whitespace.

```sh
node main.ts
```
